Page numbers make long documents easier to navigate, discuss and print. They are especially useful after merging several files into one, when the original numbering no longer makes sense. Here is how to add them in Chrome with Unique PDF.

## Step by step

1. **Open Page numbers** from the Unique PDF quick menu or the Edit section.
2. **Add your PDF.** A live preview shows how the numbers will look.
3. **Choose a format**:
   - `1`
   - `Page 1`
   - `1 of 12`
   - `Page 1 of 12`
4. **Choose the position**: top or bottom, left, centre or right.
5. **Choose where to start.** Set the first number, for example to continue numbering from a previous document.
6. **Choose which pages to number**: all of them, all but the first (for a cover page), or a custom range. You can also add a prefix or suffix and set the font, size and distance from the edge.
7. **Click Add page numbers** and download the result.

## Common setups

- **Reports with a cover page:** skip the first page and start numbering at 1 on the second.
- **Contracts and legal documents:** `Page 1 of 12` at the bottom centre, so missing pages are obvious.
- **Chapters of a book:** start at the right number so each chapter continues where the previous one ended.
- **Merged documents:** [merge the files](/blog/merge-pdf-files-free-offline) first, then add numbers to the combined PDF.

## Mixed page sizes and rotated pages

Numbers are placed relative to each page as you see it, so they land in the right corner even when a document mixes portrait and landscape pages or contains rotated scans.

## Private and free

Page numbering happens on your own computer. Your file is never uploaded, and there is no limit on the number of pages.
