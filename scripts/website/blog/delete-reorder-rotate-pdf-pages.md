Scanned documents rarely come out perfect. There is a blank page in the middle, two pages are in the wrong order and one is upside down. Fixing all three takes a minute with Unique PDF, and you never have to upload the file.

## Delete pages

1. **Open Delete pages** from the Unique PDF quick menu or the Organize section.
2. **Click the pages you want to remove.** Shift-click selects a range.
3. **Click the delete button** and confirm.
4. **Download** the new PDF.

Deleted pages are removed from the file completely, not just hidden. Their annotations, alternate text and other data attached to them go too, which matters when you remove a page because it contains something private.

## Reorder pages

1. **Open Reorder pages.**
2. **Drag the thumbnails** into the order you want. On a touchscreen, press and hold a page to drag it.
3. **Reverse the whole document** with one click if the pages are back to front, which often happens with scanners.
4. **Click Save new page order.**

You can also reorder with the keyboard: focus a page, press Space to pick it up, move it with the arrow keys and press Space again to drop it. Screen readers announce each step.

## Rotate pages

1. **Open Rotate PDF.**
2. **Select the pages** to turn, or leave everything selected to rotate the whole document.
3. **Rotate left, right or by 180°.** The thumbnails show the result immediately.
4. **Click Save rotated PDF.**

## Do it all at once with Organize pages

When a document needs several fixes, or you want to combine pages from more than one file, open **Organize pages**. It shows every page of every file you add as a thumbnail, with a colour stripe for each source file. In one workspace you can:

- drag pages into any order, across files,
- rotate single pages or a selection,
- delete the pages you do not need, and
- extract a selection as its own PDF.

Then save one new document.

## Bookmarks and forms stay intact

When you reorder, rotate or delete pages in a single document, Unique PDF keeps the document's bookmarks, forms and other features, and removes bookmarks that pointed only to deleted pages.

## Private and free

All of this happens on your own computer. There are no uploads, no page limits and no watermarks.
