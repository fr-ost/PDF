You have a 60-page report and ten minutes before the meeting. You do not need every detail; you need the main points, the key numbers and anything that requires action. That is exactly what an AI summary is good at.

Here is how to summarize any PDF in Chrome with Unique PDF's AI Summary tool, and how to choose the right kind of summary for the job.

## Choose the right summary style

Different situations call for different summaries. AI Summary offers four styles:

- **Key points** — a short bulleted list of the most important facts. The best default for reports, papers and meeting documents.
- **TL;DR** — a short overview in a few sentences, ideal when you just want to know what the document is about.
- **Teaser** — the most interesting parts, written to make you want to read more. Handy when you are deciding what to read in full.
- **Headline** — a single line that captures the main point, useful for file names, email subjects and quick notes.

You can also pick a length (short, medium or long) and the language of the summary.

## Step by step

1. **Open AI Summary** from the Unique PDF quick menu or the Unique AI section.
2. **Add your PDF** by dragging it onto the page or clicking Choose files.
3. **Pick a style, a length and a language.**
4. **Add a focus (optional).** Tell it what matters to you, for example "deadlines, costs and risks". The summary will pay special attention to those topics.
5. **Choose the pages.** Summarize the whole document, or only a range such as chapters 2 and 3.
6. **Click Summarize with AI.** The summary appears in seconds and streams in as it is written.
7. **Copy or download it.** Copy pastes clean text without formatting symbols; download saves a Markdown file you can keep with your notes.

## Long documents are no problem

Very long documents are summarized in parts, and the parts are then combined into one summary. You do not have to split the file yourself. If you only care about one section, choosing a page range gives a faster and more focused result.

## When the summary is not enough

A summary tells you what is in a document; it does not answer every question you might have about it. When you need specifics, such as "What is the penalty for late delivery?", switch to [Chat with PDF](/blog/chat-with-a-pdf-without-uploading), which answers questions and shows the page each answer comes from.

## Accuracy and privacy

AI summaries are fast, but they can occasionally miss nuance or get a detail wrong. For anything important, such as legal terms or figures you will quote, check the original pages.

Your document never leaves your computer. The text is read in your browser and summarized by the AI built into Chrome, so it is not sent to Unique Labs or anyone else. AI Summary needs desktop Chrome 138 or newer on a computer where Chrome offers its built-in AI. Elsewhere, a **Quick summary** without AI picks out the most important sentences, clearly labelled as such.

## Ideas for using AI summaries

- Get the gist of research papers before deciding which to read in full.
- Prepare for meetings by summarizing long reports into key points.
- Turn product manuals into a short list of the steps that matter.
- Summarize terms and conditions with a focus on cancellation and fees.
- Create one-line headlines to name and file documents.
