A scanner produces one long PDF of everything you fed it. A client sends a single file with ten invoices inside. A textbook PDF has the one chapter you actually need. Splitting turns one document into exactly the files you want.

Unique PDF offers four ways to split, all of them inside your browser.

## The four ways to split

- **Every page** — each page becomes its own PDF. Ideal for a batch of scanned receipts.
- **Custom ranges** — type ranges such as `1-3, 5, 8-` and each range becomes a file. Ideal for pulling out chapters or sections.
- **Equal parts** — split every few pages, for example every two pages for a stack of two-page forms.
- **At selected pages** — click the pages where a new file should start. Ideal when sections have different lengths.

## Step by step

1. **Open Split PDF** from the Unique PDF quick menu or the Organize section.
2. **Add your document.** Page thumbnails appear.
3. **Choose how to split.** Labels on the thumbnails show which file each page will go into, and they update as you change the settings.
4. **Click Split** and download. Several files download together as one ZIP archive, or you can save them one by one.

## Page range tips

Ranges are flexible: `1-3, 5, 8-` means pages 1 to 3, page 5, and page 8 to the end. You can also use words such as `last`, `odd` and `even`. If a range does not make sense, for example a page that does not exist, Unique PDF tells you right away.

## Only need some pages?

If you want one new file with just a few pages, use **Extract pages** instead: select the pages and save them as a single PDF. To remove pages from a document instead, use **Delete pages**. See [how to delete, reorder and rotate PDF pages](/blog/delete-reorder-rotate-pdf-pages).

## Links and private data

Links between pages that end up in the same part keep working, and links to pages that went into other parts are removed instead of pointing nowhere. Each part only contains its own pages: form data and hidden content from other pages are not carried along.

## Private and unlimited

Splitting happens on your own computer. There is no upload, no page limit and no watermark, and it works offline.
