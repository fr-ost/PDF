Literature reviews mean reading dozens of papers, and most of them turn out to be only partly relevant. AI can help you decide what deserves a careful read and find specific details quickly, as long as you can check what it tells you. Here is a workflow that uses Unique PDF's AI Summary and Chat with PDF.

## Step 1: Screen papers with a summary

Open **AI Summary**, add the paper and choose **Key points**. In a few seconds you get the main contributions and findings. Add a focus such as "methods, sample size and limitations" to steer the summary towards what you care about.

Use the summary to sort papers into three piles: read in full, skim, or skip. The **Headline** style is handy for writing a one-line note for each paper in your reference manager.

## Step 2: Ask specific questions

For the papers worth a closer look, open **Chat with PDF** and ask targeted questions:

- "What dataset did the authors use, and how large was it?"
- "What is the main result, with the exact numbers?"
- "Which limitations do the authors mention?"
- "How does this method differ from the baseline?"
- "What future work do they suggest?"

Every answer shows the page it comes from. Click the page reference to read the original passage, which is essential before quoting a number or citing a claim.

## Step 3: Follow up instead of starting over

The chat keeps its context, so you can dig deeper with follow-ups such as "Explain that in simpler terms" or "Which section discusses this in detail?". When you are done, export the conversation as a text file and keep it with your notes.

## What AI does well, and what to check

AI is good at finding where something is discussed, summarizing sections and explaining dense passages in plain language. It can still misread a table, mix up similar numbers or miss nuance. Treat answers as pointers to the right page, not as final facts, and always check figures and claims against the paper itself.

A few practical limits: Unique AI reads the text of the PDF, so information that exists only inside figures or images is not available to it, and scanned papers without selectable text cannot be read yet.

## Why keep papers on your own computer?

Drafts, reviews and unpublished manuscripts are confidential. With Unique PDF, papers are read in your browser and never uploaded to Unique Labs or any other server. Unique AI needs desktop Chrome 138 or newer on a computer where Chrome offers its built-in AI.
