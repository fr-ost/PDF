A watermark tells everyone at a glance what a document is: a draft, a confidential copy, a sample, or a file that belongs to your company. Unique PDF adds text or image watermarks in your browser, with a live preview so you can see the result before you save.

## Step by step

1. **Open Watermark** from the Unique PDF quick menu or the Edit section.
2. **Add your PDF.** A preview of the first page appears.
3. **Choose text or an image.**
   - For **text**, type the words, such as CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT, and choose the size and colour.
   - For an **image**, pick your logo or stamp.
4. **Adjust the look**: transparency, angle and position. Turn on tiling to repeat the watermark across the whole page.
5. **Choose where it goes**: in front of the content, or behind it so text stays easy to read.
6. **Pick the pages**: all of them, or only some.
7. **Click Add watermark** and download the result.

## Good watermark settings

- **Confidential documents:** large diagonal text at about 45°, light grey, around 15 to 25% opacity, tiled.
- **Drafts:** a single "DRAFT" across the centre of each page at low opacity.
- **Branding:** a small logo in a corner at around 30 to 50% opacity, behind the content.
- **Samples and previews:** tiled text or logo in front of the content, so it cannot be cropped out easily.

## Does a watermark protect my document?

A watermark is a visual label. It discourages copying and makes the origin of a document clear, but it does not stop someone from opening or editing the file. For real protection, add a password with [Protect PDF](/blog/password-protect-a-pdf). To remove information for good, use [Redact PDF](/blog/redact-a-pdf-properly).

## Rotated pages

The watermark is positioned on the page as you see it, so it stays upright and in the right place on rotated pages and mixed page sizes.

## Private and free

Watermarking happens on your own computer. Your document is never uploaded, there is no account, and Unique PDF never adds a watermark of its own.
