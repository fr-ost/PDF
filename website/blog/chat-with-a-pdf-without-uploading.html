How to Chat With a PDF Without Uploading It

Ask questions about any PDF and get answers with page references, right in Chrome. A step-by-step guide to private AI chat with documents.

Shahriar AhmedDeveloper of Unique PDF · November 8, 2026 · 3 min read

Long PDFs hide the answers you need in the middle of page 37. Reading the whole thing takes time, and copying chunks of it into an online chatbot means handing your document to a third party. There is a better way: chat with the PDF directly in your browser, and get answers that point to the exact page they come from.

This guide shows how to do that with Unique PDF, a free Chrome extension, and how to get the most useful answers out of it.

Why "chat with PDF" is so useful

A good document chat does three things a search box cannot:

  • It understands questions in plain language. You can ask "What happens if I cancel early?" instead of guessing the exact words the contract uses.
  • It combines information. Questions like "What are the main risks?" need several paragraphs pulled together into one answer.
  • It shows its sources. Every answer should tell you where it found the information, so you can check it in seconds.

That last point matters most. AI can make mistakes, so an answer you cannot verify is not worth much. In Unique PDF, every answer carries clickable page references such as p. 4. Click one and that page appears next to the conversation.

Step by step: chat with a PDF in Chrome

  1. Add Unique PDF to Chrome and pin it to the toolbar.
  2. Open Chat with PDF. Click the toolbar icon and pick it from the quick menu, or open Unique PDF and choose it from the Unique AI section.
  3. Add your document. Drag it onto the page or click Choose files. Unique PDF reads the text in a moment, even for documents with hundreds of pages.
  4. Ask your first question. Type it in the box, or click one of the suggested questions. The suggestions adapt to the document: a contract gets questions about obligations and deadlines, a report gets questions about the key numbers.
  5. Check the source. Click the page reference in the answer to see the original text.
  6. Keep going. Ask follow-up questions. The conversation keeps its context, so "And what about the second year?" works as you would expect.

When you are done, you can copy any answer or export the whole conversation as a text file.

Tips for better answers

  • Be specific. "What is the notice period for termination?" gets a sharper answer than "Tell me about termination."
  • Ask for a format. "List the deadlines as bullet points" or "Answer in one sentence" both work.
  • Use follow-ups instead of starting over. The chat remembers what you asked before.
  • Choose the reply language. Answers can be written in English, Spanish or Japanese, whatever the language of the question.
  • Regenerate when needed. If an answer misses the point, ask for a new one or rephrase the question.

Does it work with long documents?

Yes. For long documents, Unique AI looks for the passages that best match your question and answers from those, rather than trying to read everything at once. That is why the page references are so helpful: you can see exactly which parts of the document the answer is based on.

What about privacy?

Unique PDF never uploads your document. The text is read inside your browser, and Unique AI uses the AI built into Chrome, so your file, your questions and the answers are not sent to Unique Labs or any other server. Nothing is stored either: when you close the tool, the conversation is gone unless you exported it.

Chat with PDF needs desktop Chrome 138 or newer on a computer where Chrome offers its built-in AI. The first time, Chrome may need to set up its AI model, and Unique PDF shows the progress. On other computers, the same chat works as a clearly labelled text search that still takes you to the right pages.

Good questions to try

  • "Summarize this document in five bullet points."
  • "What are my obligations under this agreement?"
  • "Which dates and deadlines are mentioned?"
  • "What are the most important numbers in this report?"
  • "Explain section 4 in simple terms."

The bottom line

Chatting with a PDF turns a long read into a short conversation, and page references keep the answers honest. With Unique PDF it is free, it runs in your browser, and your document stays on your computer. If you would rather read a short overview first, see our guide on how to summarize a long PDF with AI.
