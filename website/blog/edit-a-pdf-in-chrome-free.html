How to Edit a PDF in Chrome for Free

Add text, highlights, shapes, arrows and images to any PDF right in Chrome — no upload, no account and no watermark. A complete guide.

Shahriar AhmedDeveloper of Unique PDF · November 9, 2026 · 2 min read

PDFs are designed to look the same everywhere, which is exactly what makes them awkward to change. You do not need expensive software to fill in a form field, highlight a paragraph or add a note, though. Unique PDF, a free Chrome extension, includes a full editor that works inside your browser.

What you can add to a PDF

The editor in Unique PDF adds new content on top of your pages:

  • Text in the size, colour and position you want, for notes, dates, names or answers on a form.
  • Freehand drawing with a pen, for circling, underlining or quick sketches.
  • Highlights over important lines.
  • Shapes: rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows, with outline colour, thickness, fill and opacity.
  • Images, such as a logo, a stamp or a photo.

Everything you add can be moved, resized, restyled or deleted, and undo and redo are always one shortcut away.

Step by step

  1. Open Edit PDF from the Unique PDF quick menu or the Edit section of the app.
  2. Add your PDF by dragging it onto the page or clicking Choose files.
  3. Pick a tool from the toolbar above the page: text, pen, highlighter, shapes or image.
  4. Click or drag on the page to add content. Use the panel on the right to change colour, size and opacity.
  5. Move between pages with the thumbnails on the left or the previous and next buttons.
  6. Click Save PDF to download the edited copy. Your original file stays exactly as it was.

Handy shortcuts

  • Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo.
  • Delete removes the selected object.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selected object for precise positioning.
  • Double-click text you added to change it.

Can I change text that is already in the PDF?

Not directly. Unique PDF adds new content; it does not rewrite the text that was already in the document. To correct a word, the simplest approach is to draw a white rectangle over it and type the new text on top. For removing sensitive text for good, use Redact PDF instead, because a white or black box alone only covers the text.

Rotated pages and large documents

The editor understands rotated pages, so text you add stays upright and lands exactly where you placed it. Large documents load quickly, and only the pages you look at are rendered, so even long files stay responsive.

Tips

  • Zoom in before placing small text so it lines up neatly.
  • Use a highlight with lower opacity so the original text stays readable.
  • If you need to sign as well, the Sign PDF tool uses the same editor with a signature pad.
  • Text uses standard PDF fonts. If a character cannot be written with them, Unique PDF tells you before saving instead of producing a broken file.

Private and free

The whole editor runs in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded, there is no account to create and nothing is stamped on your document. It even works offline.
