How to Merge PDF Files for Free (No Upload, No Watermark)

Combine several PDFs into one document in the order you want, right in Chrome. Free, private and without file limits.

Shahriar AhmedDeveloper of Unique PDF · November 8, 2026 · 2 min read

Combining PDFs is one of the most common document jobs: a cover letter plus a CV, scanned receipts for an expense claim, chapters of a thesis, or a contract and its appendices. Many online tools can do it, but they usually want you to upload your files, cap how many you can combine, or add a watermark unless you pay.

Here is how to merge PDF files in Chrome for free with Unique PDF, without uploading anything.

Step by step

  1. Open Merge PDF. Click the Unique PDF icon in the toolbar and choose Merge PDF, or open the app and pick it from the Organize section.
  2. Add your files. Drag them onto the page or click Choose files. You can add as many as you need, and add more later.
  3. Put them in order. Drag the file cards into the order you want, or use the arrow buttons on each card. The number in the corner of each card shows its position.
  4. Merge. Click the Merge button. The combined file is ready in moments.
  5. Download. Save the new PDF. Your original files are never changed.

Need to rearrange individual pages?

Merging works at the file level. If you need to mix pages from several documents, for example page 1 from one file, then pages 3 to 5 from another, use Organize pages instead. It shows every page of every file as a thumbnail, colour-coded by source file. Drag pages into any order, rotate or delete them, and save one new PDF.

Other handy tools for the same job:

  • Reorder pages to fix the order inside a single document, or reverse it with one click.
  • Delete pages to remove blank or unwanted pages before or after merging.
  • Extract pages to pull just the pages you need into a new file.

Password-protected files

If one of the files has an open password, Unique PDF asks for it when you add the file. Once you enter the correct password, the file can be merged like any other. Unique PDF never tries to guess or bypass passwords.

Why merging in your browser is better

  • Privacy. Your files are processed on your own computer and never uploaded. That matters for tax documents, contracts, medical records and anything else you would not email to a stranger.
  • No limits. No daily task limit, no maximum number of files and no watermark on the result.
  • Speed. There is no upload or download wait; even large files combine quickly.
  • Offline. It works without an internet connection, on a plane or on bad hotel Wi-Fi.

Tips

  • Give the merged file a clear name when you save it, such as 2026-expenses-march.pdf.
  • If the combined file is too large to email, run it through Compress PDF afterwards.
  • To add page numbers to the merged document, use the Page numbers tool, which can skip the cover page.
