Unique PDF is a free PDF editor for Chrome with Unique AI. Chat with any PDF, get instant summaries, and edit, merge, split, compress, convert, sign, protect and redact — without uploading a single file.

★ UNIQUE AI — CHAT WITH ANY PDF
Ask questions in plain language and get answers in seconds, with page references you can click to see exactly where each answer comes from. Ask follow-up questions, start from suggested questions, choose the reply language, regenerate, copy or export the whole conversation. Long documents work too: Unique AI finds the passages that matter.

★ AI SUMMARY
Turn a 100-page report into key points, a TL;DR, a teaser or a headline. Choose the length and language, tell it what to focus on, then copy or download the result.

★ 20 PDF TOOLS IN ONE FREE EXTENSION

ORGANIZE
• Merge PDF — combine files in any order
• Split PDF — every page, custom ranges, equal parts or at chosen pages, with ZIP download
• Organize pages — reorder, rotate and delete pages from several PDFs in one workspace
• Rotate, reorder, delete and extract pages

CONVERT
• Compress PDF — smaller files; images are optimised while text stays sharp and searchable
• Images to PDF — JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF to PDF
• PDF to images — export pages as PNG or JPEG from 72 to 600 DPI

EDIT
• Edit PDF — add text, drawings, highlights, shapes, arrows and images
• Sign PDF — draw your signature and place it on any page
• Watermark — text or image, with opacity, rotation, position and tiling
• Page numbers — several formats, positions and a custom start

SECURITY
• Protect PDF — AES-256 password encryption with permissions
• Unlock PDF — remove a password you know
• Redact PDF — permanently remove sensitive text and areas, with automatic verification
• Edit metadata — view, change or remove title, author and other details

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your files never leave your device: every tool works inside your browser. No uploads, no account, no analytics, no ads, no watermarks and no quotas. It even works offline.

★ FAST AND EASY
Drag and drop files anywhere, find any tool with the command palette (Ctrl K / ⌘ K), use keyboard shortcuts, switch between light and dark themes, and open your favourite tools from the toolbar popup. Large documents with hundreds of pages are handled smoothly.

PERFECT FOR
Students and researchers summarizing papers, professionals reviewing contracts and reports, and anyone who needs to merge, compress or sign a PDF quickly — without trusting a website with private files.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Unique AI needs desktop Chrome 138 or later on a computer that supports Chrome's built-in AI. Elsewhere, chat works as a labelled passage search and a key-sentence summary (no AI) is available.
• The editor adds new content on top of the page; it does not change text that already exists in a PDF.
• AI answers and summaries can contain mistakes. Check important details against the document.
